## Platform

web

## Users

Internship recruiters and hiring managers, deciding in under a minute whether
EJ Orr (Computer Science, University of Vermont) is worth an interview for a
Software Engineering internship. They skim: what he builds, in what languages,
and whether it is real.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio at ejorr53040.github.io/project-demos. Success: a
recruiter can open a repo, read the resume, or get in touch within seconds of
landing.

## Positioning

The projects are substantial solo systems work in Rust, not coursework
clones: a self-hosted microVM cloud, a code-intelligence index, and a
hand-tracking 3D interaction app. The page shows the real tools running.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS on GitHub Pages, served from `main` at `/`. No build step.
- Visitor actions: open each project's public GitHub repo; email
  ejorr@uvm.edu; open LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/ethan-orr); download the
  resume PDF.
- Featured projects: cirrocumulus, strata, terrain (all public). bedrock and
  devdeck are private and are not shown.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: show the running software, cite real facts.
2. Every action is one click from the first screen.
3. Nothing invented: absent evidence stays absent.
